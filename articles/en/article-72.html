Rejecting the 'Distillation' Shortcut and Acknowledging the Gap: ByteDance's Long-termism and Route Debate in LLM Strategy

From the Internal Route Debate in Seed to the 5-Trillion Parameter Behemoth: Decoding the 'Slow' and 'Fierce' Strategy of ByteDance's LLMs

Acknowledging the Gap and Setting the Route: The 'Slow' and 'Fierce' Strategy of ByteDance's LLMs

In the deep-water stage of LLM competition in 2026, ByteDance has chosen a completely different path. At the mid-year all-hands meeting on August 6, CEO Liang Rubo admitted that the company's general LLMs are falling further behind overseas leading products, proposing to accept phased technological lag, adhere to the long-term independent research route, and ensure that the rhythm of underlying R&D does not deform. This statement set the tone for ByteDance's LLM strategy: 'acknowledging the gap and rejecting shortcuts.'

Meanwhile, the latest Artificial Analysis global LLM leaderboard shows that Chinese models such as Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 Max, Alibaba's Qwen 3.8 Max, and Zhipu's GLM 5.2 Max have occupied nearly half of the top ten globally, while ByteDance's Seed 2.1 Pro is only ranked 21st. Given that ByteDance has no shortage of funds, talent, computing power, and data, why does it appear 'backward' on the leaderboard? The answer lies in the rare statement made by its founder Zhang Yiming at the Seed all-hands meeting: explicitly rejecting the use of distillation to catch up with frontier models, preferring to sacrifice short-term interests to pursue true underlying technological breakthroughs and the upper limit of intelligence. This ultimate cleanliness in technological routes is reshaping the competitive landscape of China's LLM industry.

Three 'Mutinies' and Route Debates: Why Firmly Say 'No' to Distillation?

According to insiders, the internal route debate within ByteDance's Seed team on whether to adopt 'distillation' technology has experienced at least three intense 'mutinies', all of which were firmly vetoed by Zhang Yiming. The first conflict occurred in early 2025 when DeepSeek-R1 emerged. Facing its globally sweeping reasoning style, some researchers proposed to follow up with distillation, but Zhang Yiming required Seed to 'learn how to think' like humans, rather than simply imitating 'muttering.'

The second conflict stemmed from the computing power anxiety after the deployment of NVIDIA's Blackwell chips. When competitors got the ticket to the latest computing power and ByteDance could only rely on H20 chips to struggle, the call for distillation rose again. However, Zhang Yiming said 'no' again, choosing instead to increase capital by 200 billion to build intelligent computing centers in Ulanqab and Huailai. The third was the anxiety brought by Kimi K3's entry into the global top-tier leaderboard, but in Zhang Yiming's view, the leaderboard is virtual, while commercial sovereignty and the technological base are real. These three vetoes completely ended the team's route divergence that lasted for more than a year, establishing the iron rule that both closed-source and open-source weight models are prohibited from distillation, even using technical means such as API detection to strictly investigate distillation behavior.

The 'Stubbornness' of the Underlying Technology: Guarding Against the 'Inbreeding' Trap of Synthetic Data

From the perspective of the underlying logic of technological evolution, ByteDance's insistence on training from scratch rather than being a 'distillation faction' is to avoid the 'inbreeding' trap brought by synthetic data. The essence of distillation is to use the output of other large models as 'standard answers' to train itself. Although this can significantly improve benchmark test scores within a few weeks, it hides an irreversible fatal flaw: model collapse.

A joint study that made the cover of 'Nature' in 2024 first confirmed that repeatedly training with AI-generated data will cause the tail information in the original data distribution to gradually disappear, triggering capability degradation. Meta's research at ICLR 2025 further pointed out that even mixing in just one-thousandth of synthetic data is enough to trigger model collapse. Real-world knowledge is not uniformly distributed; those rare edge cases, counter-intuitive questions, and professional knowledge in niche fields are hidden in the 'long tail', determining the upper limit of the model's ability to handle unknown problems. However, AI-generated 'standard answers' are naturally destined to flatten these long tails. ByteDance's biggest confidence lies in its possession of the world's largest native human content pool—Douyin produces over 80 million short videos daily, and TikTok covers more than 150 countries. Cleaning and denoising from PB-level real data is much more difficult than directly obtaining millions of clean instructions generated by GPT, but only native real data can avoid the quiet collapse of the model's worldview and guard the boundary of intelligence.

Betting on Ten Trillions: The 5-Trillion Parameter Behemoth and Underlying Computing Power Breakthrough

Rejecting distillation means investing a larger bet in parameter scale and computing power infrastructure. According to LatePost, ByteDance is discussing training a super-large model with a parameter scale of over 5 trillion. This scale far exceeds Alibaba's Qwen 3.8-Max (2.4 trillion) and Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 (2.8 trillion), becoming the largest known parameter model in China. The plan is led by Xiang Liang, head of Seed Foundation, and Shen Ke, head of pre-training data, both of whom come from ByteDance's core 'search, advertising, and recommendation' system.

The logic of the Seed team is: instead of struggling to catch up on the existing size, it is better to push the parameter scale to several times that of peers at once, striving for a leading position in a brute-force way. On the computing power side, facing the industry dilemma of global HBM memory shortage and limited supply of NVIDIA's high-end chips, ByteDance chooses to use heavy investment to win computing power autonomy. Tens of thousands of GPU clusters of intelligent computing centers are being rolled out across the country. This saturation attack on underlying infrastructure is the material basis for its refusal to 'copy answers' and pursuit of original innovation. Zhang Yiming comforted the team not to be overly anxious, expecting the Seed model's capabilities to eventually join the world's first tier, rather than worrying about temporary rankings.

The Commercial Logic of Long-termism: From 'Leaderboard Brushing' to Multimodal Landing and Commercial Sovereignty

While adhering to the long-termism of underlying technology, ByteDance has demonstrated strong explosive power in application-layer and commercialization landing. On August 5, ByteDance's Seed team released the native audio-video full-duplex large model SeedRealtime and fully launched it on Doubao. The model cancels the traditional ASR+TTS cascade architecture, realizing real-time multimodal interaction of 'watching, listening, and speaking at the same time'. The dialogue rhythm problem is halved compared to the cascade solution, directly benchmarking OpenAI's GPT-Live.

In addition, ByteDance's video generation model SeeDance has reached an industry-leading level. Liang Rubo made it clear at the all-hands meeting that the landing speed of AI productivity exceeded expectations, the strategic weight of To B business increased, and the company is creating a unified office productivity product matrix through organizational integration, and will cultivate Doubao as a brand-new AI backbone business after Douyin. This dual-wheel drive strategy of 'stubbornly sticking to native training at the bottom and rapidly landing multimodal applications at the application layer' proves that ByteDance's long-termism is not blind optimism, but a pragmatic move built on clear commercial sovereignty and a huge traffic ecosystem. In 2026, when AI safety warnings are frequent and frontier models are constantly testing new limits, ByteDance's strategic determination to revere technological laws and reject shortcuts may be the only rule to cross the cycle.
