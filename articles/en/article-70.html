ByteDance's AI Strategic Exam: Long-termism and Underlying Reconstruction Behind Refusing the Distillation Shortcut

In an era where 'taking shortcuts' prevails, why is Zhang Yiming determined to bet on 5 trillion parameters and native data?

The Great Exam: ByteDance's 'Falling Behind' Anxiety and Strategic Resolve

In 2026, the global large model arms race has entered a brutal deep-water zone. In the latest Artificial Analysis global LLM leaderboard, Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 Max and Alibaba's Qwen 3.8 Max both ranked in the global top ten, while ByteDance's Seed 2.1 Pro only ranked 21st. ByteDance CEO Liang Rubo frankly admitted at the mid-year all-hands meeting that the gap between the company's general large language models and overseas head products is continuing to widen. This cruel reality has pushed the internet giant, which possesses top-tier traffic and computing power, to the center of public attention. Outsiders can't help but question: with abundant funds, talent, and computing power, why does ByteDance appear to be 'falling behind' on the leaderboard?

Facing the rapid surge of domestic peers and the continuous suppression of overseas giants, there is indeed anxiety within ByteDance. However, at the Seed team's all-hands meeting, founder Zhang Yiming showed rare strategic resolve. He explicitly reassured the team 'not to be overly anxious' and set the tone: rather accept phased technological backwardness, they must adhere to the long-term independent R&D route, and the rhythm of underlying R&D must not be deformed. In a time when the 'leaderboard-only' theory prevails, ByteDance has chosen the most difficult yet most solid path, keeping its eyes firmly on the ultimate goal of the 'upper limit of intelligence,' attempting to regain the initiative in the next round of reshuffling.

Three 'Mutinies' and the Ban: Why ByteDance Refuses to be a 'Distillation Faction'?

In the early stages of model racing, 'distillation' was regarded as an industry-recognized shortcut—quickly improving its own scores by absorbing the output data of frontier large models. According to insiders, ByteDance has experienced at least three fierce route disputes over whether to follow distillation. From the sweeping global influence of DeepSeek-R1's reasoning style, to the computing power anxiety brought by the deployment of Nvidia's Blackwell chips, to the peer pressure of Kimi K3 climbing into the top leaderboard, every external shock has heightened the calls to 'take shortcuts.' Many researchers proposed to follow suit to quickly narrow the gap with the first tier, and some even regarded it as the only opportunity to overtake on the curve.

But Zhang Yiming resolutely vetoed all three. In his view, distillation is essentially copying others' capabilities; following this path can at most continuously approach opponents, but can never achieve true transcendence, and will hinder substantive breakthroughs in underlying technology. He直言 (stated bluntly) that companies of the scale of Alibaba, Tencent, and ByteDance cannot always rely on distilling others to get by. To this end, ByteDance introduced strict new regulations internally, using technical means such as API detection to rigorously investigate distillation behaviors, explicitly prohibiting the mixing of externally generated data into training sets, completely resolving the route divergence that had lasted for over a year, and establishing 'no shortcuts' as an insurmountable technical red line.

Underlying Logic: Rejecting 'Model Collapse' and Guarding 'Long-Tail Intelligence'

ByteDance's refusal to distill is not out of blind confidence, but based on a profound insight into the laws of large model technological evolution. From a technical perspective, over-reliance on distillation or AI-generated synthetic data for training can easily trigger the fatal 'Model Collapse.' Research from institutions such as Oxford and Cambridge has long confirmed that when models are repeatedly trained using AI-generated data, the tail information in the original data distribution will gradually disappear, leading to irreversible degradation of capabilities. This is like 'inbreeding' in biology; the first generation seems healthy, but after a few generations, recessive defects will concentrate and break out, ultimately leading to the quiet collapse of the model's 'worldview.'

Knowledge in the real world is not uniformly distributed; those rare edge cases, counter-intuitive questions, and professional knowledge in niche fields are all hidden in the 'long tail' of the data distribution. This long-tail information determines the upper limit of the model's ability to handle unknown problems and is the true boundary of intelligence. The 'standard answers' produced by distillation will inherently flatten these long tails, making the model increasingly confident yet increasingly narrow. ByteDance's insistence on training from scratch is precisely to avoid this trap, to guard the model's complete understanding of the complex physical world and genuine human intentions, thereby taking the lead in the true exploration of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI).

Moat Reconstruction: The Chemical Reaction of 5 Trillion Parameters and the World's Largest Native Content Pool

The confidence to reject shortcuts stems from the trump card ByteDance holds. According to reports, ByteDance is discussing training a super-large model with a parameter scale exceeding 5 trillion, led by Seed Foundation head Xiang Liang and pre-training data head Shen Ke. This scale not only far exceeds known domestic models but also points directly to the global AI frontier. Zhang Yiming explicitly stated that instead of continuing to catch up in existing sizes, it is better to push the parameter scale to several times that of peers at once to strive for a leading position. This boldness of 'brute force' is precisely built on ByteDance's awe of the laws of underlying technology.

Supporting this grand plan is ByteDance's unique data moat. Douyin produces over 80 million short videos daily, and TikTok covers more than 150 countries and regions globally, constituting the world's largest native human content pool. Cleaning and denoising from PB-level real videos, texts, comments, and interactive data is an extremely difficult engineering task, but only native real data can completely preserve the diversity of the world. Meanwhile, ByteDance has not stalled at the application layer; the SeedRealtime native audio-video full-duplex large model has been fully integrated into Doubao, achieving real-time multimodal interaction of 'seeing, hearing, and speaking simultaneously.' The 'hard work' of underlying reconstruction and the agile iteration at the application layer are jointly building ByteDance's core barriers in the next round of the AI great exam.
